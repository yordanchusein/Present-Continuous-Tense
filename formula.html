The Formula

Three patterns. Master them, and you can construct any sentence.

To Be — Quick Reference

I
am
He / She / It
is
You / We / They
are
✅ Positive
Subject + am / is / are + Verb-ing

→ I am eating pizza.

→ She is dancing on stage.

❌ Negative
Subject + am / is / are + not + Verb-ing

→ I am not sleeping right now.

→ They are not watching TV.

❓ Interrogative
Am / Is / Are + Subject + Verb-ing + ?

→ Are you listening to me?

→ Is he working today?

Adding -ing Rules

Normal
Just add -ing
play → playing
eat → eating
Ends in -e
Drop the -e, add -ing
make → making
write → writing
CVC Pattern
Double the last consonant
run → running
sit → sitting
