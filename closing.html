You're doing
it right now.

That's Present Continuous — you're literally using it just by learning English right now.

"Every expert was once a beginner who kept going."

Quick Recap

01

Function

Actions happening now, around this time, scheduled future, or annoying habits with "always".

02

Formula

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing. Negate with "not". Question: flip be to front.

03

Signal Words

Now, right now, at the moment, currently, today, this week — your clue words.

04

The -ing Rule

Add -ing normally, drop -e before -ing, and double consonant for CVC pattern.
