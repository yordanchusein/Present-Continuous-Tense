What's it used for?

Present Continuous isn't just one thing — it actually has several cool uses in everyday English.

⚡

Right Now Action

Something happening at this exact moment — you're doing it as you speak.

"She is texting her friend right now."
📅

Around This Time

Not necessarily at this second, but happening these days or this period.

"I am studying for my exams this week."
🔮

Future Plans

A scheduled or arranged plan in the near future — you've already decided.

"We are meeting tomorrow at 3 PM."
😤

Annoying Habits

Something that happens repeatedly and is irritating (usually with "always").

"He is always leaving the lights on!"

🔑 Signal Words

These words are clues that tell you to use Present Continuous:

now right now at the moment currently today this week look! listen! still
